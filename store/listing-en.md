# Gangway: Google Play listing (English)

**App name (30 max):** Gangway: Cruise Port Guide

**Short description (80 max):**
Your day ashore, sorted. Food, sights, wifi and SIMs near the pier, offline.

**Full description (4000 max):**

Step off the ship knowing exactly where to go.

Gangway is a port guide made by cruise crew for crew and passengers. It covers 319 cruise ports across the Caribbean, Alaska, Europe, Asia, Australia, South America and more, and it works offline, because ship wifi is slow and roaming is expensive.

WHAT'S IN EACH PORT
• Eat & drink: restaurants, cafés and bars near the pier, with walking times
• See & do: museums, viewpoints, beaches, forts and attractions
• Essentials: free wifi, phone and SIM shops, ATMs, currency exchange, pharmacies, supermarkets and laundry
• Port basics: local currency, whether US dollars are accepted, language, emergency numbers and plug types

OPEN TODAY, AT A GLANCE
Gangway reads each place's opening hours and tells you if it's open now, closing soon or closed today. On a Sunday it already knows which places are shut, even offline. Tap "Open now" to see only what you can visit right away.

ONE TAP TO GET THERE
Every place has a Directions button that opens Google Maps with walking directions from where you are.

NEVER MISS ALL ABOARD
Enter the all-aboard time from your daily planner and Gangway counts down for you, with a warning when it's time to head back. Tender ports are marked, so you can allow for the queue.

FIND YOUR PORT INSTANTLY
Tap "Find the port I'm in" and Gangway opens today's port for you.

STAYS UP TO DATE
Tap Refresh on ship wifi or in port to download the latest places and opening hours. Everything is then saved on your phone for the day ashore.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location never leaves your phone.
