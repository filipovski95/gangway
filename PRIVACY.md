# Plume privacy policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

Plume is a personal tracking app for people taking GLP-1 medications.

**What Plume stores.** The medication, doses, injection sites, weights, protein, water, symptoms, notes and settings you enter.

**Where it's stored.** Only on your phone. Plume has no account, no server and no cloud sync. We (the developer) never receive, see or sell your data.

**Sharing.** Plume sends nothing anywhere. If you tap "Share a summary", your phone's share menu opens and you choose who gets it.

**Notifications.** If you turn on reminders, Plume schedules them on your phone. No data leaves the device.

**Ads and tracking.** None. Plume has no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

**Deleting your data.** Use You › Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything.

**Medical disclaimer.** Plume is a tracking tool, not medical advice. Always follow your prescriber's instructions.
