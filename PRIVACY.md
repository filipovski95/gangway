# Gangway privacy policy

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

Gangway is an offline guide to cruise ports. This policy explains what the app does with information.

## What Gangway collects

Gangway has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

The only thing it sends is the port days you choose to share (see below).

## Port days shared with other crew

Not every port publishes its ship schedule, so crew can fill the gaps. When you add a port day to your ship's itinerary, or pick today's port, and **Share the days I add with other crew** is ticked, the app sends:

- the ship's name, the date, the port and the sailing time if you entered one
- a random number the app made on your phone, so the same phone counts once per ship and day

It does not send your name, email, location or anything else about you. Other users only ever see, for each ship and day, which port was reported and by how many phones; they never see the random number. Shared days are kept in a database hosted by Supabase. Days in the past are no longer shown.

You can untick the box to keep the days you add on your phone only, and removing a day you added also removes it from the shared list. To have everything your phone shared removed, email the address below.

## Location

If you tap **Find the port I'm in**, the app asks Android for your approximate location and uses it on your phone to pick the nearest port. Your location is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is not stored.

## Data stored on your phone

The app saves port guides, your last refresh time and the all-aboard time you enter, on your phone only, so the guide works without internet. Uninstalling the app removes them.

## Internet use

When you tap **Refresh**, the app downloads the latest port guides from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) and the port days crew have shared from Supabase. Those requests carry no personal information, though GitHub and Supabase, like any web server, see your IP address. When you tap **Directions** or **Website**, Google Maps or that website opens and their own privacy policies apply.

## Place information

Places come from OpenStreetMap contributors (© OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License).

## Children

Gangway is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
